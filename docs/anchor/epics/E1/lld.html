  • Htsmsg.kt + HtspClient.kt + HtspConnectionPool.kt govern message serialization, request/response correlation, and pooled connection reuse.
  • Gap: Exact framing/serialization rules and timeout/reconnect parameters require reading htsp/*.kt and HtsmsgTest.kt.
Contract

TIF Input & Channel Observation Contract

  • TvheadendInputService.kt (res/xml/tv_input.xml) bridges Tvheadend channels/EPG with Android TV's TIF framework alongside TifChannelObserver.kt and InputChannelRepository.kt.
  • Must align with docs/TIF-CROSS-INPUT-AUDIT.md and docs/boundary-contract-audit.md.
Contract

Sync Manager & Worker Contract

  • TvhSyncManager.kt, TvhSyncWorker.kt, and BackgroundSyncStrategy.kt coordinate sync execution across interactive and background contexts.
  • Gap: Concurrency policies, retry backoff, and cancellation semantics require source inspection.
Edge & Lifecycle

Server Disconnects & Reconnect Storms

  • When HTSP drops or times out, HtspConnectionState and HtspConnectionPool should transition cleanly without duplicate sockets or unguarded exceptions.
Edge & Lifecycle

Cross-Input TIF Collisions & Stale Rows

  • Concurrent updates involving TvheadendInputService, TifChannelObserver, and multi-input TIF sync must avoid overwriting or duplicating channels across inputs.
Edge & Lifecycle

Service / Worker Lifecycle & Partial Syncs

  • Handle Android service/worker teardown, process recreation, and partial EPG/DVR batches idempotently so interrupted syncs recover on next run.
